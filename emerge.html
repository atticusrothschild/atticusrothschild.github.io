Research / June 2026 – Present

Engineering at a smaller scale.

At Rice’s EMERGE Lab, I develop mechanical systems and optical test setups for printed MoS₂ microsensor research.

My role

Undergraduate Researcher · Rice University

My work connects precision mechanical design with the fabrication and characterization of microsensors for controlled drug-delivery research.

Design & testing

I designed a precision micromanipulation system and a light-tight enclosure in CAD to support repeatable positioning and optical characterization.

I also developed an opto-mechanical setup to measure light transmission and absorption through MoS₂ layers.

In the cleanroom

Hands-on microfabrication work includes photolithography, photoresist processing, and thin-film deposition.
