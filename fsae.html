Powertrain Subteam Lead

Rice Racing — Formula SAE

I lead the powertrain subteam, with work focused on fuel and cooling systems for our Honda CBR600RR-powered car. The car is currently in development.

Fuel-system layout

The design work includes selecting the pump, filtration, regulator, and hose connections, then checking how they fit into the tank and engine layout.

Radiator packaging

I’m comparing radiator mounting angles and the space required for the core, fans, and sidepod duct. The packaging study compares 45° and 60° layouts; cooling performance still requires validation.
