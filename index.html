About me

Atticus Rothschild

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Rice University who likes to design, build, and understand how things work.

I work on microsensor research in the EMERGE Lab and lead the powertrain subteam for Rice Racing. My experience spans mechanical design, cleanroom fabrication, robotics simulation, and hands-on engine work.

As a sophomore, I’m taking MECH 450 with special permission from the professor.

Outside the lab and shop, I build high-fidelity loudspeakers and experiment with amateur radio.
