Atticus Rothschild

I’m a mechanical engineering student at Rice University, interested in semiconductor equipment and manufacturing.

At EMERGE Lab, I design fixtures for microsensor testing and work in the cleanroom. Previously, at Kong Research Group, I worked on printed electronics and thermal experiments.

I also lead Rice Racing’s powertrain subteam. With the professor’s permission, I’m taking Algorithmic and AI-enabled Robotics (MECH 450) as a sophomore—an upper-level course on how robots plan motion and navigate obstacles.

My personal projects include loudspeaker design and amateur radio.
